# SkillBridge AI Business Model Canvas

## Key Partners

- Universities and career centers.
- Bootcamps and training providers.
- Student communities.
- Mentors and technical reviewers.
- Employers validating entry-level role requirements.

## Key Activities

- CV and job-description analysis.
- Skill taxonomy development.
- Personalized roadmap generation.
- Practical project recommendation.
- Portfolio evidence tracking.
- University pilot validation.

## Key Resources

- SkillBridge AI platform.
- Curated role templates.
- AI skill-extraction workflow.
- Deterministic readiness scoring engine.
- Learning roadmap and project library.
- Apex Innovators team.

## Value Propositions

- Helps students understand the gap between current skills and target jobs.
- Converts job requirements into a clear four-week learning plan.
- Encourages honest portfolio evidence instead of inflated CV claims.
- Gives mentors and career centers a practical way to guide students.
- Supports early-career readiness with transparent, explainable outputs.

## Customer Relationships

- Self-service product experience.
- Feedback-driven student pilots.
- Mentor and career-center collaboration.
- Community-based validation of role templates and learning resources.

## Channels

- GitHub portfolio repository.
- University pilots.
- Student and developer communities.
- Career-readiness workshops.
- Personal portfolio and demo presentations.

## Customer Segments

- University students.
- Fresh graduates.
- Junior job seekers.
- Bootcamp learners.
- University career centers.
- Employability and education-impact programs.

## Cost Structure

- AI API usage.
- Hosting and database infrastructure.
- Product development and maintenance.
- Security, privacy, and compliance work.
- Content creation for role templates and learning resources.

## Revenue Streams

- University and career-center pilots.
- Institutional subscriptions.
- Premium student features.
- Partner-supported learning programs.
- Custom role-template and dashboard services.
