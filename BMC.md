# SkillBridge AI Business Model Canvas

## Key Partners

- Universities and career centers.
- Bootcamps and training providers.
- Student communities and clubs.
- Mentors and technical reviewers who help validate role templates.
- Ambassadors who distribute promo codes to their own communities.
- Employers helping validate entry-level role requirements.

## Key Activities

- CV and job-description analysis (AI extraction + deterministic scoring).
- Growing and maintaining a curated, hand-written role/skill taxonomy across software,
  design, data, marketing, security, product, and IT tracks.
- Personalized roadmap generation, adjusted to a student's available weekly hours.
- Practical portfolio project recommendation and evidence tracking.
- Skill quizzes and weekly check-ins to keep learners on an adaptive plan.
- Resume/CV bullet generation from logged, real evidence.
- Manual payment verification (InstaPay) and subscription/entitlement management.
- Promo code, campaign, and ambassador program administration.
- Internal team administration: role-based access control, invites, and audit logging
  for anyone who manages the platform.
- University pilot validation and role-template feedback loops.

## Key Resources

- The SkillBridge AI platform (Next.js/React web application).
- The curated role-template library (skills, priorities, projects, resources — 15+
  roles at launch of this expansion, actively growing toward broad CS/tech coverage).
- The AI skill-extraction and roadmap-generation workflow (Google Gemini).
- The deterministic readiness-scoring engine, independent of AI output.
- The evidence, reassessment, quiz, check-in, and resume-builder feature set.
- The subscription/entitlement, promo code, and manual-payment infrastructure.
- The admin dashboard and RBAC system for safely delegating platform operations.
- The Apex Innovators team.

## Value Propositions

- Helps students understand the real gap between their current skills and a specific
  target job — not a generic "learn to code" recommendation.
- Converts job requirements into a clear, time-boxed learning plan matched to how much
  time the student actually has each week.
- Encourages honest portfolio evidence instead of inflated CV claims, and lets a
  student watch their readiness score improve as they build real proof.
- Covers a broad and growing set of career tracks, not just software engineering, so
  it's useful across a CS program's realistic range of outcomes.
- Gives mentors and career centers a practical, explainable tool to guide students.
- Supports early-career readiness with transparent, auditable, non-AI-hallucinated
  scoring.

## Customer Relationships

- Self-service product experience, from sign-up to roadmap to evidence tracking.
- Feedback-driven student pilots that shape new role templates.
- Mentor and career-center collaboration.
- Community-based validation of role templates and learning resources.
- Direct support for payment verification and account issues (manual InstaPay review).

## Channels

- The SkillBridge AI website (skillbridge.ai-equivalent deployment on Vercel).
- GitHub portfolio repository.
- University pilots and career-readiness workshops.
- Student and developer communities.
- Ambassador-driven referral and promo code distribution.
- Personal portfolio and demo presentations.

## Customer Segments

- University students across CS and tech-adjacent majors.
- Fresh graduates.
- Junior job seekers targeting software, design, data, marketing, security, product, or
  IT/support roles.
- Bootcamp learners.
- University career centers.
- Employability and education-impact programs.

## Cost Structure

- AI API usage (Google Gemini).
- Hosting, database, and email-notification infrastructure.
- Product development and maintenance, including ongoing role-content curation.
- Security, privacy, and compliance work (RBAC, audit logging, data handling).
- Payment verification overhead (currently manual, via InstaPay).

## Revenue Streams

- **Starter** — a monthly subscription for students actively closing gaps (more
  analyses, full roadmap, curated resources).
- **Pro** — the primary paid tier: unlimited analyses under fair use, job-specific
  roadmaps, the Portfolio Evidence Builder, and reassessment.
- **Annual** — Pro's full feature set billed once a year at a discount versus paying
  monthly.
- **Job Sprint** — a one-time, 30-day access package for a student targeting one
  specific role and deadline.
- Promo codes and ambassador/referral-driven conversions.
- Future: university and career-center institutional pilots or subscriptions.
