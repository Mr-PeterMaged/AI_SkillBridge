# SkillBridge AI Proposal

## Project

**SkillBridge AI** is a career readiness copilot for students and fresh graduates.

## Team

**Apex Innovators**

## Impact Area

Education and employability.

## Problem

Many university students and fresh graduates do not know why they are not ready for a target role or what they should learn next. They often follow random courses without a clear connection to job requirements, while employers expect practical technical and soft skills that may not be visible in a student's CV or portfolio.

SkillBridge AI turns this confusing gap into a clear, actionable plan.

## Solution

SkillBridge AI compares a student's CV or profile text with a real job description, extracts structured skills, highlights matched and missing requirements, calculates a transparent readiness indicator, and generates a personalized four-week learning roadmap.

The roadmap focuses on practical skill development and portfolio evidence, such as GitHub repositories, live demos, case studies, and project deliverables.

## AI Usage

AI is used to:

- Extract technical and soft skills from the user's CV or profile.
- Extract structured requirements from a target job description.
- Identify gaps between the user's current profile and the target role.
- Generate a personalized four-week learning roadmap.
- Recommend practical mini-projects that help the user prove new skills honestly.

The readiness indicator is deterministic and should not be treated as a hiring decision.

## Target Users

- University students.
- Fresh graduates.
- Junior job seekers.
- Bootcamp learners.
- University career centers and mentors.

## Initial Roles

- Junior Frontend Developer.
- Junior Backend Developer.
- Junior Data Analyst.

## Expected Impact

SkillBridge AI helps learners understand what employers need, what skills they already have, what skills they are missing, and what projects they can build to close the gap between education and employment.

## Safety and Ethics

SkillBridge AI provides educational career guidance only. It does not guarantee employment, make hiring decisions, or encourage users to fabricate skills, credentials, projects, or work experience.
