# SkillBridge AI — User Guide

This guide walks through how to use SkillBridge AI as a student or job seeker, from
your first analysis to tracking your progress over time. A short section at the end
covers the admin dashboard for platform owners/team members.

## 1. Create an Account

1. Go to the SkillBridge AI homepage and click **Sign Up** (or **Get Started**).
2. Sign up with your email or a supported social login.
3. You'll land on your **Dashboard**, which starts empty until you run your first
   analysis.

## 2. Run Your First Skill-Gap Analysis

Click **New Analysis** from the dashboard. This is a 3-step wizard:

### Step 1 — Career Goal

- **Target role**: use the search box to find your role (e.g. "frontend", "data",
  "marketing") or browse by category. Roles are grouped by track (Software
  Development, Data, Design, Marketing & Growth, Security, Product & Program, Content
  & Documentation, IT & Support, Quality & DevOps).
- **Experience level**: Student, Fresh Graduate, or Junior.
- **Weekly study hours**: how many hours per week you can realistically commit. This
  directly changes how long your roadmap will be — more hours per week means a shorter,
  more intensive plan.

### Step 2 — Your Profile

- Upload a PDF or DOCX CV (max 5MB), **or** paste your CV/profile text directly.
- Your CV is used only to generate your analysis — it is never used to train a public
  model, and you can delete it at any time from Settings.

### Step 3 — Target Job

- Paste a real job description from a role you're interested in. This gives far more
  accurate, personalized results than a generic template.
- No job posting handy? Click **Use a template** to load a realistic template for your
  selected role, or **Fill demo inputs** to see the whole flow with sample data.
- Review the summary at the bottom, then click **Analyze My Skill Gap**.

## 3. Review Extracted Skills

Before anything is scored, SkillBridge AI shows you the skills it extracted from your
CV so you can correct it — AI extraction isn't perfect, and a wrong or missing skill
would make your score wrong too.

- Remove anything that was extracted incorrectly.
- Add any skill you have that wasn't picked up.
- Adjust your mastery level per skill (Familiar / Practiced / Proven) where relevant.
- Click **Confirm** when the list looks right.

## 4. Understand Your Results

The results page shows:

- **Readiness score** — a deterministic percentage computed by SkillBridge AI's own
  scoring engine, not by the AI model. The same inputs always produce the same score.
- **Matched, partial, and missing skills** — organized by priority (critical /
  important / nice-to-have) against your target job.
- **Top priority gaps** — the skills worth focusing on first, with a plain-language
  reason why each one matters for the role.

This score is a learning and preparation indicator, not a hiring decision.

## 5. Follow Your Roadmap

Your personalized roadmap breaks the gap-closing work into weekly chunks:

- Each week has a focus area, a learning objective, curated resources, and specific
  tasks.
- Mark tasks complete as you finish them — this is what your progress tracking is
  based on.
- Roadmap length adjusts automatically based on the weekly hours you set in Step 1.

## 6. Build Real Proof (Portfolio Evidence)

*(Pro, Annual, and Job Sprint plans)*

- **Portfolio Project Builder** suggests specific mini-projects tied directly to your
  missing skills, complete with deliverables and a GitHub checklist.
- **Evidence Builder** lets you log real proof against a skill — a GitHub repo, a live
  demo link, a case study, or a written reflection.
- **Reassessment** recomputes your readiness score once you've logged new evidence, so
  you can see your score improve as you build real proof — not just because you claimed
  a skill.

## 7. Check Your Understanding (Skill Quizzes)

Go to **Quizzes** from the dashboard nav to take short, supportive checks on
student-level fundamentals for specific skills. Quiz results modestly support your
confidence signal, but real portfolio evidence remains the stronger proof of a skill.

## 8. Stay on Track (Weekly Check-ins)

*(Pro, Annual, and Job Sprint plans)*

Weekly check-ins let you report actual progress against your roadmap. If you're ahead
or behind, your plan adapts instead of staying static and unrealistic.

## 9. Build Your Resume

As you log evidence, SkillBridge AI can turn it into honest, specific CV bullet points
— phrased the way a recruiter expects to read them, without inventing anything you
didn't actually do.

## 10. Track Your Applications

Use the **Applications** page to keep a simple record of where you've applied and what
stage each application is at, so nothing falls through the cracks.

## 11. Plans and Billing

Go to **Billing** (or **Pricing** from the homepage) to see the available plans:

| Plan | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Free** | One analysis per month, a locked roadmap preview. |
| **Starter** | More analyses per month, the full roadmap, curated resources. |
| **Pro** | Unlimited analyses under fair use, Portfolio Evidence Builder, reassessment, full history. |
| **Annual** | Everything in Pro, billed once a year for less than paying monthly. |
| **Job Sprint** | A one-time, 30-day package for one specific role and deadline — not a subscription. |

### How to upgrade

1. Choose a plan and click its button. If you have a promo code, enter it and click
   **Apply** to see the discounted price before you commit.
2. Fill in your **full name** (in English) and **phone number** — this is how the team
   matches your InstaPay transfer to your account.
3. Click **Create payment request**. You'll get a Payment Request ID and the InstaPay
   number to transfer the exact amount to.
4. Send the transfer confirmation via the **Open WhatsApp** button (pre-filled with
   your request details) so the team can verify it.
5. Your plan activates once the payment is manually verified — check **Billing** for
   your request's status (Pending, Under Review, Approved, or Rejected).

SkillBridge AI will never ask you for your OTP, PIN, card number, or banking password
— never send those to anyone claiming to represent SkillBridge AI.

## 12. Manage Your Account

From **Settings** you can:

- Delete individual analyses.
- Delete your account and all associated data.

## 13. Admin & Team Access

If your account has been granted a platform role (Owner, Admin, Marketing Manager,
Support Manager, Analyst, or Viewer), you'll see an **Admin** link in the navigation
bar. This opens a separate dashboard for:

- Reviewing and approving/rejecting manual payment requests.
- Creating and managing promo codes, campaigns, and ambassadors.
- Inviting and managing team members and their roles.
- Viewing platform analytics and the admin audit log.

What you can see and do inside the admin dashboard depends on your specific role —
for example, a Viewer can look but not act, while an Owner has full control, including
transferring ownership. If you believe you should have admin access but don't see the
link, contact the account owner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the readiness score a guarantee I'll get the job?**
No. It's an educational indicator of how well your current, verifiable skills match a
specific job description — never a hiring decision.

**Will SkillBridge AI make up skills or experience I don't have?**
No. Every suggestion is designed to help you build or prove skills honestly. It will
never fabricate credentials, projects, or work history on your behalf.

**What happens to my CV after I upload it?**
It's used only to generate your personal analysis. It is never used to train a public
model, and you can delete your analyses (and the CV text stored with them) at any time.

**My target role isn't in the list — what do I do?**
SkillBridge AI's role library is actively growing beyond its original 3 roles toward
broad coverage of software, design, data, marketing, security, product, and IT/support
career tracks. If your role genuinely isn't covered yet, use the closest matching role
or check back as the library expands.

**Why do I need to submit an InstaPay transfer manually instead of paying by card?**
Payments are currently verified manually while the team validates demand before
integrating an automated payment processor. This means activation isn't instant — it
happens once the team confirms your transfer.
